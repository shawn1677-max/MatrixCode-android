# Privacy Policy for Codefall

**Last updated: 26 August 2026**

Codefall does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. None.

## What the app does not do

- It requests **no Android permissions**.
- It has **no internet access**. The app does not declare the `INTERNET`
  permission, so it is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere, to us
  or to anyone else.
- There are **no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no
  third-party SDKs** of any kind.
- It contains **no trackers** and no unique identifiers.

## What is stored on your device

Your settings — colour theme, character set, speed, trail length, the optional
message text, and the other display options — are saved in Android's standard
private app storage so the app remembers them between launches. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not readable by other apps,
- is deleted when you uninstall Codefall.

## Sensors

If you turn on **Tilt steers the rain**, the app reads the device's gravity
sensor while the rain is on screen, purely to decide which way the code should
lean. Sensor readings are used for that frame and immediately discarded. They
are never recorded, stored, or transmitted. Reading motion sensors at normal
rates requires no Android permission. The sensor is unregistered as soon as the
rain is no longer visible; the feature is off by default.

## Children

Codefall contains no ads, no purchases, no user-generated content and no data
collection, and is suitable for all ages.
