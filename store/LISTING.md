## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Codefall turns your phone into a wall of falling code.

Tune it to taste and watch it live: eight colour themes, six character sets, and
sliders for speed, glyph size, column density, trail length and glyph churn. The
preview at the top of the settings screen updates as you drag, so you can dial in
the look before you commit to it.

Run it three ways:

- Fullscreen screensaver, launched from the app. Tap anywhere to exit.
- System screensaver (daydream), so Android starts it while your phone is
  docked or charging.
- Live wallpaper behind your home screen, running at a lower frame rate to stay
  easy on the battery.

FEATURES

- Eight colour themes: Classic Green, Amber CRT, Ice Cyan, Red Pill, Neon
  Violet, Gold, Ghost White, and a Rainbow mode that sweeps hue across the
  columns and drifts over time.
- Six character sets: Katakana, Binary, Hexadecimal, ASCII, Symbols and Mixed.
- Mirrored glyphs for a more authentic look, switchable.
- Tilt steering: turn it on and the rain leans toward whichever edge of the
  phone you tip down.
- Secret messages: type any text and the code periodically resolves into it,
  holds for a moment, then dissolves back into noise.
- Optional clock, in 12 or 24 hour format.
- CRT scanlines, glow and glitch effects, each independently adjustable.
- Randomise button for looks you would not have dialled in by hand.

PRIVACY

Codefall requests no permissions at all. It has no internet access — the app
cannot send data anywhere because it never asks for the ability to. No ads, no
analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, no trackers. Your settings
are saved on your device and are deleted when you uninstall.

If you switch on tilt steering, the gravity sensor is read only while the rain is
on screen, only to decide which way the code leans, and the readings are never
stored or sent anywhere.

Works on Android 5.0 and later.
```
